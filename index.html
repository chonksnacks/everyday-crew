Our Story

Started with
a bad sock.

Not catastrophically bad. The kind you get comfortable with — cotton that pills after a few washes, elastic that marks your calves but gives out by month two, the pair that disappears somewhere between the washer and the dryer. We're two people in New York who decided that wasn't good enough. So we made the sock we couldn't find anywhere else. The best version of the thing you put on every single morning without thinking about it. Because when it's right, you don't have to.

Material Composition
  • 85% Combed Cotton Combed before weaving to strip out the short, rough fibers — the ones responsible for pilling, that scratchy feeling, and why regular socks look a year old by their third wash.
  • 10% Nylon Built into the high-wear zones. The pair holds its shape wash after wash, not just the first few.
  • 5% Spandex Just enough stretch for a fit that stays put. Snug without grip marks. Structured, but soft.
